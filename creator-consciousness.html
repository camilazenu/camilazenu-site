Your Guide

Meet Camila

Camila is a Somatic Practitioner, embodiment coach, and creator consciousness mentor. She works with people who feel called to share their voice but can't seem to get out of their own way, because she spent years there herself.

Born in Colombia, raised in California as a DACA recipient, she built the streetwear label DeadBlud, appeared on HBO Max's The Hype, and when both her business and her marriage ended in the same season, she went inward. What emerged is the work she now leads with women around the world.

The Framework

The CREATOR Method

Seven pillars for embodied, sustainable content creation

C

Conscious Identity

Claim who you are as a creator before you ever post a single piece of content. Build from your essence, not your ego.

R

Regulate the Nervous System

Your body holds the key to your voice. Learn somatic tools to move from freeze to flow before you create.

E

Express Lived Experiences

Your stories are not liabilities. They are your greatest creative assets. Learn how to transform them into connection.

A

Align with Purpose

Create from a place of meaning, not metrics. Discover the through-line that makes every piece of content feel inevitable.

T

Tell Your Story

Master the art of narrative: structure, vulnerability, and the sacred balance between raw truth and intentional craft.

O

Own Your Voice

Stop sounding like everyone else. Identify your distinct creative fingerprint and protect it fiercely.

R

Repeat Through Consistency

Sustainable creativity is built on rituals, not willpower. Design a creative life that holds you even when inspiration fades.

The Curriculum

Five Calls of Deep Transformation

Each call builds on the last, an intentional spiral inward and outward.

Call 1

Why Do You Want to Use Your Voice?

Identity, intention and the deeper why behind your calling.

  • Identity excavation and personal story mapping
  • Dismantling old beliefs about who gets to speak
  • Defining your core message and mission
  • Grounding your why in purpose, not performance
Call 2

Excavating Your Values

The values that anchor you, and the why that fuels every word as we go on the excavation journey.

  • Naming your core values and the stories behind them
  • Why your values are the compass for every piece of content
  • Aligning your voice with what truly matters to you
  • Using your values as a filter for what to share and what to protect
Call 3

Knowing Your Worth

Worthiness, lived wisdom and the truth that you are already enough.

  • Deep worthiness and identity work
  • Brand pillar creation from lived experience
  • Story to content framework exercises
  • Releasing the need to perform or prove yourself
Call 4

The Fear of Being Seen

Your nervous system, the body's fear response and your first authentic post.

  • Nervous system regulation tools
  • Somatic practices for visibility fear
  • Visibility challenges, your authentic content goes live
  • Using cringe as a portal, not a wall
Call 5

Testimony vs. Oversharing

Embodied truth, authentic expression and your lifelong practice.

  • Embodied storytelling frameworks
  • Performance vs. authentic expression
  • Your repeatable expression practice beyond 30 days
  • My personal content system for Instagram and TikTok
