Breathwork, somatic work and your health

I’m a creator, somatic practitioner and mentor. My work isn’t medical, psychological or any other professional treatment, and it doesn’t replace care from a licensed provider.

Breathwork can bring up strong sensations and emotions. Check with your doctor before you join breathwork if you’re pregnant, or if you have a heart condition, high blood pressure, epilepsy or seizures, glaucoma, a recent surgery or injury, or a history of panic attacks or psychiatric conditions. Go at your own pace, and slow down or stop whenever you need to. You choose to take part, and you’re responsible for your own wellbeing while you do.
