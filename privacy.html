Privacy

What I collect when you sign up, join, book or pay through this site, who helps me look after it, and how to see, change or delete it.

Last updated November 24, 2026

The short version.

  • I collect only what you give me, and what’s needed to run your membership, your sessions and my newsletter.
  • I never sell your information, and I don’t share it for advertising.
  • This site runs no advertising or tracking scripts.
  • To see, change or delete what I have, write to me at camila@camilazenu.com.

Who I am

This site is run by me, Camila Zenu, in California. I decide how the information on this page is used, and I’m the person to ask about it.

What I collect, and why

When you join my newsletter (the sign-up that pops up on the site): your name, your email and the page you signed up on. I use them to send you my newsletter. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

When you join a waitlist: what that form asks for, such as your name and email, so I can tell you when doors open.

When you book a call or a session through Calendly: your name, email, the time you chose and your answers to the booking questions. I use them to hold the session and prepare for it.

When you create an account for Creator Academy or my programs: your name, your email and a password. Your password is kept by my login service, and I never see it. Your account also records what you’ve joined, which libraries you can open, whether you said you’d come to a free call, and the state of your membership.

When you pay: Stripe takes your card details. I see your name, your email, what you bought and whether the payment went through, never your full card number.

When you post in the members’ area: your posts and comments, shown with your name to the members who can see that space. You can delete your own comments.

When you join a live call: calls happen on Zoom and are recorded. The recordings, with the names, faces and voices of the people who spoke or had their camera on, go into the replay library for members. To stay out of a recording, keep your camera off and change the name Zoom shows.

When you write to me: your message and your email address, so I can answer you.

When you visit: like any website, the services that host this site and serve its fonts and videos receive your internet address and basic browser details, to deliver the pages and keep the site safe from abuse.

How I use it

  • To give you what you signed up or paid for: the newsletter, your sessions, your membership and your recordings.
  • To keep my records of who has joined what, including when a membership starts, renews, pauses or ends.
  • To answer your questions and send you notes about your membership or programs.
  • To keep my payment and tax records, as the law requires.

Who helps me

A few trusted services handle information for me. Each one uses it only to do its job for me, under its own privacy policy:

  • Netlify hosts this site, runs the logins and keeps the member records.
  • Stripe processes payments and memberships.
  • Zapier moves newsletter sign-ups and membership changes into my records.
  • monday.com keeps my records of sign-ups, clients and members, and runs my waitlist form.
  • Kit sends my newsletter.
  • Calendly handles bookings.
  • Zoom runs the live calls.
  • Cloudflare stores and plays the replay videos.
  • Google serves the fonts the site uses.

I may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect someone’s safety. I don’t share your information with anyone for their own marketing.

Cookies and your browser

This site doesn’t run advertising or tracking scripts. It uses two small things in your browser:

  • a login cookie, only once you log in, so the site knows it’s you;
  • a note that you closed the newsletter sign-up, so it doesn’t keep popping up.

Calendly’s booking window loads only when you choose to book. The Stripe checkout and the waitlist form are those services’ own pages, and they may use cookies to work and to prevent fraud. Because this site doesn’t track you from site to site, there’s nothing for a browser’s “Do Not Track” setting to turn off.

How long I keep it

I keep your information while you’re on my newsletter, a member, a client, or waiting to hear back from me. After that I delete it, except what the law asks me to keep longer, such as payment and tax records.

Your choices

  • Newsletter: unsubscribe anytime with the link at the bottom of any email.
  • Membership: cancel anytime from the Account tab in My Academy.
  • See, correct or delete your information, or close your account: write to me at camila@camilazenu.com. I’ll answer within 30 days, usually much sooner. I may ask you to confirm it’s you first.

Keeping it safe

I use well-known, secure services for logins, payments, records and email, and only I and the people who help me run my business can see your information. No system is perfect, so please don’t send card numbers or passwords by email.

Adults only

My programs and this site are for adults. I don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If I learn that I have, I delete it.

Changes to this policy

If I change how I handle information, I’ll update this page and the date at the top. If a change is a big one, I’ll say so on the site, and email members.

Contact

Questions about your information? Write to me at camila@camilazenu.com. The rules for using the site are in my Terms.
